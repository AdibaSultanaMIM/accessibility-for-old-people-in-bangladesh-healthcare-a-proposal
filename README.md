# Accessibility-for-old-people-in-bangladesh-healthcare-a-proposal
Toward Accessible and Trust-Calibrated LLM-Mediated Medical Information for Bangladeshi Older Adults

Large language models (LLMs) are becoming increasingly common for finding and understanding medical information. However, older
adults may not benefit from these technologies in the same way as younger users, especially in low-resource and multilingual settings.
In Bangladesh, older adults may face several challenges, such as limited digital literacy, age-related accessibility difficulties, complex
medical terms, lack of trust, and difficulty understanding AI-generated information. This study looks at how older adults in Bangladesh
access, understand, evaluate, and interact with LLM-based medical information in Bangla. Rather than focusing only on the technical
performance of the AI, we focus on what older adults need to use these systems more easily, understand the information, develop
appropriate trust, and use the information safely. Our study follows a three-phase mixed-methods approach. First, we will conduct
semi-structured interviews to learn about older adults’ current ways of finding medical information, their accessibility challenges,
trust expectations, and how they decide whether information is reliable. Second, we will use participatory co-design to understand
their preferences for language, interaction, response format, explanations, uncertainty, verification, privacy, refusal, and referral to
healthcare professionals. Finally, we will evaluate different LLM-based interaction designs through usability and accessibility testing.
We will look at how well participants understand the information, whether they rely on AI appropriately, how they verify information,
how accessible they find the system, how successfully they complete tasks, and how much mental effort is required. Bangla medical
scenarios will be used throughout the study with appropriate safety measures because healthcare is a sensitive area. The main outcome
of this study will be a set of evidence-based design requirements for making LLM-based medical information more accessible and
trustworthy for older adults in Bangladesh. This study aims to contribute to HCI research on aging and accessibility by focusing on
the real needs of older adults and supporting safer use of AI-based health information in a low-resource language setting.
